About PaFaqe.al

PaFaqe.al is built specifically for web design agencies, digital marketers, and freelance developers looking to connect with local businesses across Albania that do not yet have an online presence.

Many small businesses—ranging from cafés and retail shops to auto repair and hotels—rely solely on foot traffic or social media. PaFaqe.al aggregates open data to pinpoint these exact businesses, providing accurate contact details, categories, and lead scoring so you can pitch your web development services effectively.
